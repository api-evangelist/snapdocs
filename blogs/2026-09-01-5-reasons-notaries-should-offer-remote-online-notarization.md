The way people complete important transactions is changing. Mortgage closings were some of the first to go digital, and that shift hasn't slowed down — RON now has permanent legal authorization in 45 states plus D.C. for real estate, making it one of the fastest-growing ways to get real estate transactions signed, sealed, and delivered.
