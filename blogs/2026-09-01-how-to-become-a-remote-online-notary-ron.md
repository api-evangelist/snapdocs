How to Become a Remote Online Notary (RON) in Your State Notaries have always been the last line of trust in a real estate closing — the person in the room making sure signers are who they say they are, and that the paperwork is legally sound. As more of that work moves online, Remote Online Notarization (RON) is becoming one of the fastest ways for notaries to grow their business, serve more clients, and get paid for the expertise they already have.
